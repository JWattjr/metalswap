# Ready-to-paste Portal description

MetalSwap is a focused GenLayer StudioNet prototype for one bounded question: over a 15-minute UTC interval, does Gold or Silver deliver the stronger relative return? It uses one pair and demo-credit pari-mutuel mechanics—no leverage, AMM, or order book.

Public app: [metal-swap.vercel.app](https://metal-swap.vercel.app) · Repository: [github.com/JWattjr/metalswap](https://github.com/JWattjr/metalswap) · Exact deployed source: [`f21a67be03e7d40dd68cff63a38de1f31d3ad2e1`](https://github.com/JWattjr/metalswap/commit/f21a67be03e7d40dd68cff63a38de1f31d3ad2e1). MetalSwap: `0x3D5ccf7e160031EC049c52815283269b95D8DbD7`. SettlementGate: `0x5b00b98fa2a530E1b0E676b96A2FEC74012AFb20`.

The [wallet-free completed market proof](https://metal-swap.vercel.app/comparison/xaus-live-2026-09-16-12-15-00z) records market `market-2026-09-16T12:15:00Z`. Both sides were funded with 25 demo credits before cutoff. Validators independently fetched XAUS's public XAU/USD and XAG/USD series and accepted Gold `4345.100098 → 4341.299805` and Silver `64.767998 → 64.733002`. Both fell; Silver outperformed because it fell less (about `-0.0540%` versus `-0.0875%`). Exact integer cross multiplication selected SILVER, the SettlementGate finalized, the winner received 49 credits after a 1-credit fee, a duplicate claim rolled back, and the claimed position remained readable after rotation. The page links the settlement, gate, payout, duplicate-claim, and rotation receipts.

The policy is frozen before entry: `XAUUSD` and `XAGUSD`, USD per troy ounce, fixed-point scale `1,000,000`, latest observation at or before each boundary, maximum boundary staleness `180s`, and maximum cross-metal timestamp skew `60s`. Validators check successful bounded HTTP/UTF-8/JSON transport, exact instrument/unit fields, freshness and ordering, positive bounded prices, alignment, canonical hash agreement, and then deterministic contract arithmetic. Missing, malformed, stale, conflicting, non-2xx, truncated, or oversized evidence stays pending and becomes a fee-free refund after the deadline. No LLM performs the arithmetic.

XAUS describes these observations as indicative mid-market values, not official benchmarks, settlement-grade data, or executable prices. Demo credits have no cash value. This proves a real externally published observation flow and testnet mechanics, not fair real-money trading. The earlier [synthetic proof](https://metal-swap.vercel.app/comparison/metalswap-synthetic-2026-09-14-07-30-00z) is preserved and explicitly labeled predictable; delayed publication does not make synthetic outcomes unpredictable. Direct tests mock HTTP evidence, while the linked StudioNet receipts and readbacks are the live demonstrated behavior.
