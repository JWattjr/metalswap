# Ready-to-paste Portal description

MetalSwap is a focused GenLayer StudioNet prototype for a narrow question: over a 15-minute UTC interval, does synthetic Gold or Silver deliver the stronger relative return? Users select GOLD or SILVER and stake demo credits in a pari-mutuel pool. After expiry, validators independently retrieve one frozen four-observation record, agree on its exact canonical fields, and deterministic contract code compares relative returns by integer cross multiplication. A separate SettlementGate keeps claims disabled until the matching finality payload is recorded.

Live mechanics demonstration: [public app](https://metal-swap.vercel.app), [repository](https://github.com/JWattjr/metalswap), [completed market evidence](https://metal-swap.vercel.app/evidence/market-2026-09-14T07:30:00Z.json). Deployed source revision: `6a31ea0ca02d7f08ae8c67f7fa6cd1384d4e05c1`. MetalSwap: `0x04d331073ba620FC165Cf7841e71e9F1270f44c7`. SettlementGate: `0x7EfCc55ccD29Eb63bf727e5b5213a1D44c450759`.

The observed market funded both sides with 25 demo credits, expired at `07:45:00Z`, settled to SILVER from the public synthetic record, finalized in SettlementGate, paid 49 credits, rejected a duplicate claim, and remained inspectable after owner-controlled rotation to the next market. Exact receipts and readbacks are in [`deploy/DEPLOYMENT.md`](https://github.com/JWattjr/metalswap/blob/main/deploy/DEPLOYMENT.md).

Validators verify the frozen HTTPS URL, successful transport, bounded UTF-8 evidence bytes, strict schema, exact boundary timestamps, conservative numeric limits, canonical SHA-256 hash, consensus agreement, finality-gate authentication, and deterministic fee/refund/payout conservation. The synthetic evidence generator is public, deterministic, and predictable; demo credits have no cash value. This is a controlled mechanics demonstration—not fair trading, live-price verification, or real-money trading. Direct regression tests use mocked transport; the deployment, settlement, finality, payout, duplicate rejection, rotation, and historical readback were demonstrated live on StudioNet. Real-price trading is not ready.
